Assimilate.simulate! — Method

Simulate a sequence of states x_seq and observation mean vectors ȳ_seq for model given a sequence of control variable vectors v_seq. The lengths of x_seq and v_seq should match, with x_seq[t] the model state immediately prior to updating with control variable vector v_seq[t] at time index t. The length of y_seq should be an integer multiple of the length of v_seq and x_seq, with this integer multiple corresponding to the subwindow length between pairs of control variable updates to the model state. States and observation means are written in place to x_seq and y_seq and model is updated in place.

Assimilate.simulate_subwindow! — Method

Simulate a subwindow of observation times starting at index t for model, updating its state in place using control variable vector v and simulating for as many observation times as there are entries in ȳ_subseq, with the observation means for each time index being written in place to ȳ_subseq.
